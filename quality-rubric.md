# Quality rubric — generated post concept

Applies to one concept card (archetype + one-liner + caption + CTA + hashtags + poster + scores) as shown on `/content/[eventId]`. Score each concept yes/no against all five.

1. **Facts check** — Does every claim (dates, history, figures) trace back to the event's researched brief, with nothing invented?
2. **Archetype distinctiveness** — Is this concept's message, tone, and visual direction genuinely different from the other two concepts for the same event (not a reworded duplicate)?
3. **Cultural/sensitivity fit** — For Islamic/Pakistani/religious events, does the copy respect the flagged do's/don'ts, and is the sensitivity flag shown when the event requires SME sign-off?
4. **Copy completeness & polish** — Are one-liner, caption, CTA, and hashtags all present, on-brand in tone, and free of grammar/clarity issues?
5. **Brand & visual consistency** — Does the poster use the brand's palette/logo, and is the text/logo placement legible and on-brand?

**Verdict:** a concept passes only if all five are "yes." Any "no" on #1 or #3 blocks approval outright, regardless of the other four.
