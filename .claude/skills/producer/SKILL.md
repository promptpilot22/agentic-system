# Task
For ONE `ConceptBrief`, produce both its `CopyPackage` (one-liner, caption, CTA, hashtags) and its `VisualSpec` (palette, layout, image prompt), matched to the event's audience and the brand's tone.

# Guardrail
Never use a color, font, or tone outside the brand-guidelines skill's stated palette/fonts — brand constraints hold even when an event has its own strong visual identity (e.g. a national flag's colors).
